African Print Materialities

A digital-first archival platform exploring the material, visual, and discursive life of African print cultures — zines, pamphlets, journals, artists' books, and printed ephemera. Part archive, part bibliography, part visual essay, part pedagogical tool.

01 — About

Print as Material Practice

Rather than simply documenting objects, the project foregrounds print as material practice — paper, ink, texture, typography, circulation, marginalia, and modes of production — as critical to how knowledge is produced, reproduced, and transmitted across African and diasporic contexts.

Core Aims

To index and document a growing personal collection of African print and textual artifacts; to create a public-facing archive that resists institutional gatekeeping; to develop a discursive space where texts are interpreted, contextualized, and reactivated; and to experiment with media as a scholarly and curatorial medium.

Platform Logic

The digital archive functions as a grid catalog and index, with carousels operating as "micro-zines" and captions serving as critical annotations. Visual culture is treated as process — handling, flipping, narrating, assembling — foregrounding tactility and voice across recurring thematic clusters.
